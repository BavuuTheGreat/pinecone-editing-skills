# Pinecone reel format taxonomy (v1, from the Oct 2026 audit of 32 reels)

Classify every reel into exactly one format. Use the tie-breaker order below
when a reel mixes formats: pick the first one that matches.

| # | Format | Signals in the contact sheet | Typical length / pace | Oct 2026 baseline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Motion / CGI teaser | No camera footage; 3D type, renders, starfields; event date card | 10 s, <3 cuts/min (long animated shots) | 2 reels, median 12.4K views, 0.52% like rate |
| 2 | Cinematic brand film | Staged actors + CGI/surreal shots, letterboxed or 16:9, film grade, no talking to camera | 35-155 s, 20 cuts/min | 3 reels, median 13.2K, 0.07% (likely paid) |
| 3 | Event / partnership film | Stage, crowds, handheld mic interviews, co-brand lockup (e.g. MongolZ x Pinecone) | 55-190 s, 18 cuts/min | 4 reels, median 10.7K, 1.20% |
| 4 | Presenter / program promo | Staff host on camera in office, lower-third or list cards, CTA end card | 30-205 s, 8 cuts/min | 4 reels, median 7.0K, 0.25% |
| 5 | Podcast clip | Two people with headphones + mics, word-by-word captions, podcast title bar | 65-165 s, 3 cuts/min | 10 reels, median 8.1K, 0.90% |
| 6 | Creator collab | External creator, selfie or vlog camera, outdoor/home, creator's own caption voice | 20-155 s, 5 cuts/min | 9 reels, median 30.4K, 1.71% |

Tie-breakers: (a) a podcast cut used inside a promo is format 4, not 5;
(b) a creator filmed at a Pinecone event is format 6 if the creator posted it
as a collab, else 3; (c) any reel with >= 50% generated/rendered screen time is 1 or 2.

Add a new format only when 3+ reels fit none of the above. Record it here with the same columns.

Added in the Nov 2026 audit (100 reels, 25 May - 5 Nov 2026):

| # | Format | Signals in the contact sheet | Typical length / pace | Nov 2026 baseline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | Seated interview / testimonial | Students or a guest expert seated in a studio set (sofa or desk), lav mic or handheld mic, word captions, question text cards; no headphones (else 5), not staff hosting (else 4) | 60-185 s, 4 cuts/min | 8 reels, median 13.5K organic, 0.51% |
| 8 | Office trend / staff skit | Staff doing a trend format (dance, photo-frame, "who is the impostor"), phone camera, #lifeatpinecone caption | 15-115 s, 2 cuts/min | 5 reels, median 9.0K, 0.79% |

Nov 2026 baselines for formats 1-6 (organic medians, boosted reels excluded): 1 Motion 9.1K (6 reels), 2 Brand film 12.8K (3), 3 Event 10.0K (10), 4 Presenter 8.9K (17), 5 Podcast 10.1K (30), 6 Creator collab 36.6K (21).
