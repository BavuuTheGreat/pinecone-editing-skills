# Engineers matter more with AI (series)

One engineer talks straight to camera about building something with AI; the screen recording of what they built (or the
reel that inspired it) slides in beside them. Pick it for any episode of the series "Engineers matter more with AI".
Episode 1 is the office-guide story: the team's 3D office guide, built after seeing a 3D tour reel.

**Status:** Beta (episode 1 made 2026-11-16, awaiting review) · **Made by:** `testimonial-reel/engine/engineers.py` + `src/eng/*` (Remotion)
**Audit:** the series has no history. Account recon 2026-11-17 (@pineconemongolia, 36 reels on the page): most reels sit at 3-8K views; the big numbers are almost all boosted (75K at 141 s with 66 likes, 22K at 102 s with 71 likes, 14K at 16 s with 50 likes, 13K at 164 s with 120 likes); the one organic hit is **243K, 58 s, 3.4K likes** (a collab with a well-known esports team). Earlier audit (100 reels): the best organic median is the office-trend format, 36.6K, with a face or a bold headline from frame 0. Lesson used for the 30 s cut: short, a person on frame 0, one clear problem, no long preamble. Watch percentage is not public.
**Examples:** none posted yet.

## Looks (job `look`)
**`split` (default, "Clean Split", 2026-11-17):** the reviewer asked for the series to be simpler and in Pinecone's own clean aesthetic, like a reference reel she sent (analysis: `engineers-series/references/reference-reel-analysis.md`). The demo (map, screen recording, reel) sits **edge to edge across the top, 1080 x 575**, the presenter fills the area under it (face at 36 % of that area), the region grows from the top edge in 20 frames and closes the same way; no cards, rounded corners, shadows, borders or blurred room. Chapters are **not shown** (review 2026-11-17: "get rid of the sections such as Санаа, they should not come up on the screen"; job `show_chapters: false` is the default, `true` brings back the plate callout in split look / the pill in cards look). Earlier a chapter was announced by a **black plate callout** (white bold Manrope 46 px, 14 px radius, the name resolving out of green noise, 2.4 s) at the top of the presenter area; labels ("@handle", "Office guide") are small black plates on the demo's lower-left corner; the faded Pinecone mark hides while the demo is up. Kept from the first version: Ghost Text intro, hard cuts, the light leaks, GIP captions, the map, scene-matched demo, jazz bed, loudness. **`cards`:** the earlier look (rounded cards over a blurred room, chapter pill). Everything below describes both; where they differ the rows say so.

## The idea (why it looks like this)
An engineer types; the AI suggests; Tab accepts. Every part of the theme is that one gesture:
- **Intro**: "Engineers matter" is typed (cream), "more with AI" appears as grey **ghost text** with one shimmer, a **Tab** keycap is
  pressed, the ghost turns solid left to right and "<more/>" goes solid, "more" in Pinecone green italic. The caret leaves, the first shot opens out of the black.
- **Captions**: each word starts as ghost text and turns solid as it is spoken.
- **Sign-off**: the title returns and a ghost suggestion ("EP 02 · soon") types itself and is *not* accepted: that is the follow prompt.
One accent colour (Pinecone green). Nothing else glows, flashes or spins.

## Signals (pick this theme when)
- The footage is one presenter talking to camera (any length 60-110 s) plus screen recordings of a product, site or reel they mention
- The story is "we saw X, we built Y with AI" and the viewer should see Y
- It is an episode of the series (the title and ep number are the series identity; see Need from you)

## Need from you
- ★ presenter name and role spelled exactly (episode 1: "<Presenter Name>", "Engineering Manager", confirmed 2026-11-17; shown as the name plate and the closer signature)

## Recipe
1. **Look at the footage.** `ffprobe` each file (phone clips are often 1920x1080 with a rotation tag: ffmpeg auto-rotates, the
   engine needs nothing), contact sheets (`reel-audit/scripts/contact_sheet.sh`), and the speech envelope (the room can be
   quiet: Episode 1 speech is -33 to -38 dB, room tone -48 dB; `speech_segments()` thresholds relative to the room).
2. **Transcribe with the reel generator.** Extract the talk as m4a (`ffmpeg -ss <start> -t <len> -i take.mov -vn -ac 1 -ar 44100 -af highpass=f=80,loudnorm=I=-16 -c:a aac -b:a 96k talk.m4a`, under 10 MB), upload it at `dash.pinecone.mn/reel` (browser tools, signed-in Chrome; the file input is `type=file`, then **Transcribe**), read the lines with the JS in `testimonial-reel/references/transcription.md`, add the m4a start offset to every time, correct per `captions.md`, and put unsure words in the report (episode 1, all confirmed: "Google Maps-ийн", "Claude болон Higgsfield-ийг", "Event hall", "Pinecone-д", "50 метр" (confirmed by the presenter; generator said 10, whisper 5), "хэдүүлээ"; unreadable words go in `[hidden brackets]`). Whisper is only a tie-break and for locating inserts: `whisper-cli -m ~/.cache/whisper-cpp/ggml-large-v3-q5_0.bin -l mn -ml 1 -sow` for word times, used to find where
   the presenter mentions the reel / the demo (episode 1: "reel" ~32.9 s, tools ~40.7 s, Google Maps ~48 s, "let's look" ~62.7 s).
   Real captions: the two-pass `dash.pinecone.mn/reel` transcription, corrected by a person (see the PineQuest card, step 2).
3. **Write the job.** Copy `testimonial-reel/engine/batches/engineers/_example.json` to `<job folder>/job.json`: `speech` span, `screens` (crop
   the reel and the page content only), `stacks`, `chapters`, `captions` (corrected text), `end_card`.
4. **Align.** `python3 -I engineers.py job.json --align` (one-time `setup.py --align`). Needs `pieces` = the speech span. Re-run after every text edit.
5. `python3 -I engineers.py job.json --plan`: read the timeline (takes, stacks in presenter time). Then render:
   `python3 -I engineers.py job.json` (render concurrency 2; about 10-15 min for 100 s on a 16 GB Mac).
6. **Check** (below). Stills at: intro frames 20/64/92, first shot opening (f 128), a full shot, each insert (mid), the sign-off, the dissolve
   into the end card.
