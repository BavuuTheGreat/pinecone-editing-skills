# Creator collab

An outside creator's vlog-style video about Pinecone, in the creator's own voice. Best performing type.

**Status:** Beta · **Made by:** `assemble.py` (light touch) · **Audit:** 21 reels, median 36.6K organic (3x any other type), 1.71%, 20-155 s, 2-10 cuts/min
**Examples:** instagram.com/reel/DdIipF8yn6i (149K), /reel/DdJdvJoBe7P, /reel/DbswhuzuC0T

## Signals
- Selfie or vlog camera, outdoor/home, the creator talks to their own audience

## Need from you
- ★ The creator's handle and name · ★ the creator's OK on the final cut · optional: their edit notes

## Style
| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| Length | Keep the creator's length; trim only dead air |
| Structure | The creator's story order. Don't restructure |
| Pace | The creator's own (2-10 cuts/min) |
| Captions | The creator's caption style if they have one, else `WordCaptions` |
| Graphics | Minimal: handle sticker, at most a logo sting. No Pinecone list cards |
| Music | The creator's choice (licensed), else none |
| End | Pinecone end card only if the creator agrees |

## Recipe
Edit lightly: cut dead air and retakes, fix audio level, captions, reframe to 9:16 if needed.
It must still look like the creator made it; that is why it works.
