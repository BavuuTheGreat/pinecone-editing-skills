# engineers-series - "Engineers matter more with AI"

The theme card is the rulebook: `../edit-reel/references/themes/engineers-matter-ai.md` (style table, recipe, checklist,
skills inventory). The renderer is `../testimonial-reel/engine/engineers.py` + `src/eng/*` (Remotion) +
`reelpipe/eng_music.py`. This skill is the entry point and the memory of how episode 1 was made and what the reviewer rejected.

## Before the first episode on a computer
1. `python3 ../testimonial-reel/scripts/setup.py` (python 3.10+, numpy, node, ffmpeg, Remotion `npm ci`).
2. `python3 ../testimonial-reel/scripts/setup.py --align` (optional, ~0.5 GB venv + 1.2 GB model): forced alignment of captions. Needed for exact caption timing.
3. Brand pack (not in the public repo; ask the Editing manager, `setup.py --brand`): `assets/GIP-Regular.woff2` (caption face), `assets/pinecone_mark.svg` (faded top mark + intro lockup), `assets/end_card_9x16.mp4`. All three are optional: without them captions use Manrope, the mark is skipped, the episode ends on the sign-off.
4. For captions: Chrome signed in to `dash.pinecone.mn` (the reel generator) and the claude-in-chrome tools. Helpers (subagents) normally cannot do this step: do it in the main session.

## Make an episode
1. **Look at the footage**: `ffprobe`, contact sheets (`../reel-audit/scripts/contact_sheet.sh`), speech envelope (the take may start with an empty room). Phone clips are often 1920x1080 with a rotation tag: ffmpeg auto-rotates.
2. **Ask for what is missing** (never guess): presenter name + role spelled exactly, episode number, which sentence each screen recording belongs to, credit for any third-party reel shown, chapter names (3 short words each).
3. **Find the screen-recording crops**: the page content only (no browser tabs, no URL, no usernames or comments). Find the stretch with real motion by frame differencing and note its start time and length (`screens.<id>.t`, `.seconds`). Look at the FIRST frame of every grab: it is on screen while the window opens.
4. **Captions** (`../testimonial-reel/references/transcription.md`, `captions.md`): extract the talk as m4a (<10 MB), upload at `dash.pinecone.mn/reel`, transcribe, read the lines with the JS snippet, add the m4a start offset, correct the Pinecone mishears (Pinecone, Claude, Higgsfield, Google Maps, Academy, App, Event hall), put unreadable words in `[hidden brackets]`, collect unsure words and ask the presenter. Then force-align.
5. **Write the job** from `../testimonial-reel/engine/batches/engineers/_example.json` into a job folder outside the repo (real jobs are git-ignored).
6. `python3 -I engineers.py job.json --align`, then `--plan`, then render; `--intro` renders the standalone intro; `--remix` redoes sound only.
7. **Check** with the card's checklist: stills at the intro, the leak, each chapter, each insert (first frame too), the sign-off; read every caption line against the audio; loudness line.
Commands (from `testimonial-reel/engine/`): `python3 -I engineers.py <job> [--plan|--intro|--align|--remix]`. Render concurrency 2 (16 GB Mac); a 100 s episode takes 10-15 min.

## The 30-second cut (current format since 2026-11-17)
The reviewer said the videos are too long and watch percentage too low, so an episode is now **30 s max**: face on frame 0 with a problem hook (no intro), the built-with-AI line, the solution demo over his words, one resolving sentence, and the Ghost Text title as the **closer** (the old intro moved to the end; the sign-off and the brand end card are gone). Job keys: `name_tag` ({name, role, from, to}: the plate on the first seconds + the closer signature), `target_seconds` (30: the closer stretches so the video lands exactly on it), `opening: "none"`, `closer: true`, `clips` (source seconds; edges snap to a breath; hard cuts + 12 ms fades), `stack_move_frames: 10`, `curtain_frames: 12`, `speech_over_bed_db: 12`, `caption_style: "gip"`, `leaks_at` (one green leak into the built-with-AI line), a short map layer with `cues.start/legDur`. How to cut a new episode: write the transcript with times, pick 5-7 sentences (problem x2, how it was made, 3-4 solution lines, one resolution), list them as `clips`, give each scene of the demo the `at` of its sentence, `--align`, `--plan` (read the printed total: must be <= 30 s), render, then the self-review below. The 109 s version of episode 1 is `job.before-30s.json`.

**Self-review checklist for every cut** (including: read the audio energy at EVERY clip edge, no word clipped at either end, the last word of the video fully audible before its fade) (what was checked on episode 1): total duration <= 30 s and loudness -14.5 LUFS / <= -1.4 dBTP; face on frame 0; every clip edge in a breath (no click: first-difference of the audio at each cut); captions inside the clips only, trimmed to speech; no black gap while a demo region opens or closes (region and presenter share one progress; the last layer lives MOVE+2 frames past its end); speech clearly above the music (about 12 dB) and the music rising when speech ends; the leak never over a caption word; the closer holds about 1 s after Tab; no leftover sign-off or end card; stills at 0 s, each cut, each leak, the demo scenes, the last frame.

## What the look is (summary; the card has the numbers)
**Two looks.** `"look": "split"` (default since 2026-11-17, "Clean Split", after the reviewer asked for something simpler and in Pinecone's own aesthetic; analysis of her reference reel in `references/reference-reel-analysis.md`): the demo sits edge to edge across the top (1080x575), the presenter fills the area under it, chapters are announced by a black plate callout, nothing is rounded, shadowed or blurred. `"look": "cards"`: the earlier rounded-card layout over a blurred room. Everything below applies to both unless it says otherwise.

- Ghost Text intro (serif, typed, ghost `<more/>` / `with AI`, Tab key, accept; `more` in Pinecone green, brackets white; nothing else on screen); the first shot arrives under a pink light leak. One accent colour: Pinecone green `#00D37F`.
- Talk: hard cuts inside pauses (few, ~4 per 90 s), no camera movement, chapter pill that swaps in place, light leaks only where needed (job `leaks_at`): Pinecone green where the story starts, pink/purple at the two chapter changes.
- Inserts (split look): a region grows from the top edge, full width; presenter below. (cards look: the presenter eases into a card over the blurred room; a wide window or a tall diptych.) The reference reel carries the creator's @handle. The wide window can hold layers one after another: a black-and-white A-to-B map (B = Pinecone Academy, the last 50 m dashed) and then the screen recording cut into scenes that follow the sentences.
- Captions: white slim serif (Source Serif 4) on a tight black plate (like the reviewer's reference reel), hard cut on/off, bottom of the screen (y 1620). No chapter labels on screen.
- Faded Pinecone mark top-centre. Sign-off: title returns, ghost "EP 02 · soon" types and is not accepted; brand end card dissolves in.
- Sound: a supplied jazz track ("Jazz Music #1", Mogo Mogo; job `music_file`; check the licence and credit it) or, without one, the synthesized 108 BPM swing-jazz bed; soft typed keys in the intro and sign-off. Master -14.5 LUFS by static gain + limiter.

## Animation library (Annnimate starter pack)
`animations/README.md` catalogs the 19 free components the reviewer supplied (raw copy in `animations/annnimate-starter-pack/`, git-ignored, third-party) and how each could be used in a video. Five are ported to Remotion in `testimonial-reel/engine/src/eng/anm.tsx` and used or ready: `CurtainOut` (curtain-slider: 12 columns wipe between the layers of the wide window), `DualScramble` (chapter label and the map's "Pinecone Academy" resolve out of green noise), `SplitFlapTile` (the "50 m" tag), `ChapterRail` (optional `chapter_style: "rail"`), `TextReveal`. Rule: keep it simple, one or two animations per episode where they explain something. To add another, follow "How to port another one" in that README.

## History: what was tried and rejected (do not bring back unless asked)
Round 1 (first build) had a window-opening reveal, dissolve+push on every cut, ink-panel chapter wipes: "transitions repetitive, don't fit". Round 2 rotating ffmpeg transitions (zoomin crops the face, fadeblack jarring) and a subtle camera: "camera weird, black screen at section change". Round 3 hard cuts, no camera, light leaks on 4 cuts: "pop up in unnecessary parts". Round 4 plain captions in a black chip: "slim, no black background", then "like the testimonial reels". Sound: noise-based clicks/air swells ("sandy"), 88 BPM arpeggio bed ("more laid back, jazz"). Typed-key sounds were removed once and then asked back, subtle and tonal. Episode 2 (2026-11-18, presenter talks about the editing system): same look, 32.0 s, `brand_moments` also take `name: "text"` callouts (`label`, `color`) for the key numbers/terms ("2-3 цаг" cream, "5-10 минут" green, "Software Engineers") plus the Claude Code logo moment; music = Funkorama by Kevin MacLeod (CC BY 4.0, `engine/assets/music`, `music_start` 8.5, `speech_over_bed_db` 10); stacks show the Marketing Planner video-review screen recording.
