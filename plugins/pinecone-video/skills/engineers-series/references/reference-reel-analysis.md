# Reference reel: what the reviewer wants the series to look like (analysed 2026-11-17)

The reviewer said the video should be **simpler and in Pinecone's own aesthetic (simple, straightforward, clean)** and pointed at an Instagram reel
(instagram.com/p/Dfrmh7UoG8k, the Annnimate starter-pack promo by @juli_fella, 37.4 s, 1080x1920). It was watched in the browser by seeking
and taking stills at 0.8, 5, 10, 16, 24, 31 and 36.4 s (nothing downloaded).

## What it does
| Part | Observed |
|---|---|
| Layout | **Vertical split.** The demo (website / UI screenshots / scrolling galleries) sits **edge to edge across the top, about 30-35 % of the height**; the presenter fills the rest, head and chest, face about a third of the way down that area. No cards, no rounded corners, no borders, no shadows, no background blur. At the very end the demo closes and the presenter is full-frame. |
| Demo content | Real product screens, changing every 2-4 s on the beat of the sentence, never decorative. |
| Headline callouts | A few words in **bold white sans on a solid black plate** (corner radius about 10-14 px), at the boundary between demo and face: "The most premium library on the web", "19 free animations", "Motion". On screen about 2-3 s each. |
| Captions | 2-4 words, **white serif (Times-like) on a tight black plate with square corners**, lower third over the chest, hard cut on and off. |
| Transitions | **None to speak of**: hard cuts between demo clips. No light leaks, wipes, glows or flashes. |
| Colour | Black, white, neutral. All colour comes from the demo content and the presenter's natural-light room. No brand-colour overlays, no gradients. |
| Sound | Voice only in the screen recording; no visible sound design. |

## How episode 1 was adapted ("Clean Split", job `look: "split"`, the default since 2026-11-17)
Kept from the first version (the reviewer and Editing manager liked them): the typed **Ghost Text intro**, the hard-cut talk, the **pink and Pinecone-green light leak at the
intro hand-over and where the story starts**, the **GIP captions** (Pinecone's own caption style; plain, no box), the **black-and-white A-to-B map**, the **demo scenes matched to the sentences**,
the Instagram handle credit, the swing-jazz bed with soft typed keys, the loudness master, the faded Pinecone mark on the full-frame talk.
Changed to follow the reference: the demo is an **edge-to-edge 1080x575 region at the top** (the recordings' own 1.877 aspect, so nothing is cropped) and the presenter fills the area under it;
the rounded cards, the blurred room, the card shadows and the chapter pill are gone; **a chapter is announced by a black plate callout** (white bold sans, name resolving out of green noise, 2.4 s);
the handle and "Office guide" labels are small black plates on the demo's lower-left corner; the Annnimate curtain wipes the demo region between its layers.
The earlier look stays available: job `"look": "cards"`.
